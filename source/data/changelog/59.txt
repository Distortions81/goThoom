Multi-session:
- Open up to ten independent character tabs above one shared game view. Use + to add a tab and its X, with confirmation, to disconnect and close it.
- Connect from each tab with saved characters or the built-in demo character, and use the game view's logout button to disconnect without closing the tab.
- Switch tabs with the mouse or customizable Ctrl-1 through Ctrl-9 and Ctrl-0 hotkeys. Rendering, input, shared windows, scripts, and audio follow the selected character.
- Keep background sessions connected and processing network updates, macros, Go scripts, recordings, reconnects, and separate Text Logs without rendering their scenes.
- Resume a still-active bard performance at its current time when its tab is selected.
- Restore open tabs and the selected tab on the next launch without reconnecting automatically.
- Share one settings configuration across every character and session tab.

Bard music:
- Match the classic client instrument program and percussion behavior, including the supported instrument aliases and octave handling.
