goThoom 49 changes since test48.

Interface:
- Reorganized the main Settings window into focused Display, Text, World, and Speech & Alerts tabs, making the settings easier to browse without an extremely wide window.
- Added consistent Material icons throughout the interface, including movie playback, toolbar actions, setup, settings, scripts, shortcuts, groups, login, and common confirmation controls. Context menus and dropdown rows can now align icons alongside their labels.
- Added a dedicated Snapshot icon to the toolbar and improved its spacing.

Startup and asset loading:
- Added a short startup precache for common, quick-loading artwork and sounds found across the bundled movies. This includes frequently used magic effects, interface and profession artwork, and common held items, reducing first-use pauses during movie playback and normal play.
- Common sound loading runs alongside artwork preparation, and managed-atlas uploads are deferred until each artwork batch has finished its CPU work.
- Greatly reduced image-archive parsing time and allocation pressure by reading resource tables and metadata in contiguous chunks instead of thousands of small operations.
- Replaced the per-bit image decompressor with a buffered indexed-image decoder, reusable bounded pixel buffers, packed palette lookup tables, and wider pixel stores. Image masks and transparency checks share the same decoder.
- Improved palette expansion, denoising, sprite upscaling, and fixed eight-tap high-quality sound resampling with measured lookup-table, word-at-a-time, and selectively unrolled operations.

Rendering and hitch reduction:
- Reduced managed texture-atlas churn by keeping cached artwork managed while moving temporary render targets and evicting speech-bubble text images to standalone textures.
- Removed an explicit garbage collection after room artwork preparation and replaced large temporary artwork allocations with bounded reusable buffers.
- Avoided unnecessary Players-window refreshes and persistence work when repeated backend who records do not change visible or saved player state.
- Reuses wrapped Chat and Console message layouts until the window width or font changes, avoiding repeated measurement of the full message history as new text arrives.
- Reduced active-window update work as well: Chat and Console now append retained rows incrementally, the Players window limits artwork work to visible rows and checks recent-player expiry at most every 10 seconds, Inventory reuses stable row actions, and animated speech bubbles composite only their bounded screen regions.
- Closed Inventory, Players, Chat, Console, and About windows now defer content work until they reopen. The Players window retains unchanged rows and headers instead of recreating their image items and reloading artwork, and unchanged held items reuse their existing toolbar composites.
- Correctly deallocates cached sheets and processed artwork when settings invalidate them, while retaining stable cache entries during normal rendering.
- Added optional per-frame asset diagnostics with `-assetLoadTrace`, reporting artwork decoding, processing, upload submission, managed and unmanaged image activity, atlas cache clears, scale-factor changes, and slow draw frames.
- Custom-colored mobiles now share one CPU-upscaled base pose and a palette-independent influence mask; their small palettes are applied on the GPU before animation-frame blending. This avoids caching and uploading a separate managed texture for every player's colors while retaining the CPU artwork upscaler and the existing denoise/shader-disabled fallback. The small uncolored source sheets for named mobiles found across the bundled movies are warmed at boot without eagerly allocating their much larger scaled pose and mask caches.
- Reduced shader submission overhead by selecting only the nearest light-cone shadows before sorting, using constant-time replacement-effect dispatch, avoiding redundant shadow scratch-image copies, and drawing with native 32-bit index buffers.
- Reduced high-refresh rendering work by reusing unchanged server-state snapshots, caching static speech-bubble bodies in bounded standalone textures, and bypassing the multi-pass layered-shadow compositor when character shadows do not overlap.
- Reduced first-use artwork hitches by splitting large single-frame denoise and CPU-upscale work across otherwise idle art workers, while making the denoise edge detector and transparent-pixel handling cheaper without changing processed pixels.
- Window focus is now sampled at most twice per second and shared by input, notifications, and power-saving checks instead of repeatedly querying the window backend.
- Refreshed the compiler optimization profile with a five-minute VSync-off, full-quality movie-rendering workload capped at 300 FPS and covering the current shader, atlas, vector, and interpolation paths.

Developer and diagnostic tools:
- Added `-dumpPreloadAssets` to export the exact startup-preloaded images and sounds under `dump/preload` for inspection.
- Added `-framePacingTrace` to report slow full-frame intervals split into Update, Draw, draw-state lock wait, and presentation/driver/scheduler time, with nearby garbage-collection timing.
- Added an optional `-pgoMaxFPS` ceiling for reproducible VSync-off profiling on software renderers that would otherwise submit thousands of redundant frames per second.
- Expanded regression coverage for chunked archive parsing, indexed-image decoding, reusable buffers, atlas diagnostics, startup precaching, and tabbed interface behavior.
