goThoom 52 changes since test51.

Rendering and artwork loading:
- Reduced drawing overhead for window title-bar grip marks.
- Cached rendered text across window repaints to reduce repeated work in Chat, Console, and other text-heavy windows, while preserving regular, bold, and italic text.
- Reduced unnecessary shadow-compositing work when character and artwork shadows are separated, while preserving overlapping shadows and drawing order.
- Reduced sprite-upload data by skipping unused texture padding. Uploads remain batched to avoid spreading loading work across multiple frames.
- Removed redundant staging copies for tightly packed sprites uploaded into fresh or same-size reused texture slots.
